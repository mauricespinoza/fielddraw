Working scale

Locked, the map pans but does not zoom, and the scale is held as you move north or south — the denominator drifts with latitude on its own. Pinch zoom and two-finger rotate are off while it lasts.

0.28 mm is the OGC standard pixel — the same assumption QGIS and ArcGIS make, so a 1:25 000 here is their 1:25 000. It is not the true pixel of any particular screen: to make the scale match a ruler held against this one, adjust until the scale bar measures what it says.

    Line symbology

    Colour, size, spacing and position of faults and fold axial traces. The colour swatch repaints both the trace and its ornament. Position is the perpendicular distance to the trace: the sign picks the side, and flip in the properties menu mirrors the symbol across the trace on one feature, sending it to the other block. Fold arrows straddle the axis, so they have no side to flip.

      Strike and dip symbols

      The strike bar is drawn along the measured strike and the tick points down-dip (right-hand rule), so the symbol reads the same here, in QGIS and in StraboSpot. Below 3° the horizontal symbol is used and the tick disappears: a dip that small cannot state a direction. Above 87° the vertical one, with a tick on both sides.
